Privacy and accessibility

Draft for Wilson Conveyancing to review before use.

What this website collects

Nothing. The quote form and calculators run in your browser. When you press “Send on WhatsApp”, your answers open in WhatsApp and you choose whether to send them. There are no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.

When you contact Wilson Conveyancing

Messages, calls and documents you send are used to give you a quote and, if you instruct us, to act on your property transaction, including identity and anti-money-laundering checks required by law. They are kept for as long as the law and our regulator, the Council for Licensed Conveyancers, require. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, and complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Accessibility

This site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA: it works with a keyboard and screen readers, respects reduced-motion settings and keeps text contrast high. If something doesn’t work for you, call or WhatsApp +44 7830 315714.
